{config.displayName} details for dealerships, companies and fleet operators around {config.primaryCity} and the Treasure Coast. Volume work is a steady part of what we do, alongside owners of some of the highest end cars in the area.

## How it is priced

On volume and turnaround, not per vehicle off a menu. A lot that needs twenty cars turned around before a weekend and a company with six vans on a monthly rotation are different jobs with different numbers, and pretending otherwise with a single per-vehicle price would be wrong for one of you.

## On your lot or at the shop

The [detailing packages](/services/mobile-detailing) are mobile, so working through vehicles on your lot is normal. Anything that needs controlled conditions, which means [ceramic coating](/services/ceramic-coating), [paint protection film](/services/paint-protection-film) and [paint correction](/services/paint-correction), is done at the shop.

## What we need to know

How many vehicles, what sort of state they are usually in, how often, and what you need them to look like at the end. A lot preparing cars for the line wants something different from a service company that wants its vans not to look neglected, and the quote reflects which one you are.
