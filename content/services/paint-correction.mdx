{config.displayName} corrects paint for owners across {config.primaryCity} and the Treasure Coast. Correction is machine polishing: swirl marks, wash marring, light scratches and oxidation are cut and refined out of the clear coat until the finish has its depth back. It is done at the shop in {config.primaryCity}, because what gets corrected is decided by what the lighting shows.

## It starts with a paint evaluation

We look at the paint first and identify what is actually there: scratches, oxidation, and the surface defects that are flattening the gloss. That evaluation is what the quote is built from, which is why correction is not priced before the vehicle is seen.

## Correction and polishing

The defects are levelled and the paint refined back to a clean finish. How far that goes depends on the paint: a one step polish lifts the lighter defects in a single pass, while deeper swirls and oxidation need multi step work that cuts further and then finishes finer.

## Then protect it, or it goes straight back

Correction removes a thin amount of clear coat to level what is in it, and a car does not have an unlimited supply of clear coat. Locking the result in with a [ceramic coating](/services/ceramic-coating) is what stops the next few months of washing putting the swirls right back where they were. It is recommended rather than required, and we will say so either way at the evaluation.

## A final detail and inspection

A complete finishing pass and an inspection under the lights before the vehicle goes back to you.
