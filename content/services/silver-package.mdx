{config.displayName} brings the Silver Package to you anywhere in {config.primaryCity} and the surrounding towns. It starts at two hundred twenty-five and includes everything in the Bronze Package plus an exterior wax and a light interior detail.

## What does Silver add over Bronze?

Everything the Bronze Package covers, then an exterior wax over the paint and a light interior detail. It is aimed squarely at the owner who wants the outside of the vehicle looking its best rather than simply clean, which is why it is the most commonly booked of the three.

## Is the full interior included?

No, the interior work here is light: a vacuum and a wipe down. If the cabin needs a deep clean, seat shampoo, pet hair pulled out or odor removal, that is the [Gold Package](/services/gold-package), which details the interior properly rather than freshening it.

## Can you add a clay bar?

Yes. A clay bar treatment pulls embedded contamination out of the paint before the wax goes on, and it can be added to a Silver Package. Ask for it when you book and we will quote it alongside the rest of the job.

## How long does the wax last?

A wax is a short-term layer measured in weeks, and the sun and rain down here shorten it further. If you want protection measured in years rather than weeks, that is a [ceramic coating](/services/ceramic-coating) rather than a wax.
