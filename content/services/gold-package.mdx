## What makes the Gold Package different?

It includes everything in Bronze and Silver, then adds a full exterior detail and a full interior detail that reaches the parts of a vehicle most cleans never touch. The paint is detailed meticulously, and the job finishes with a protection blend that holds for at least forty-five days.

## Can you get out pet hair, stains and sand?

Yes. Seat shampoo, pet hair and the sand that gets into everything around here are all part of this package, and odor removal comes with it. If the interior is the reason you are booking, this is the one that fixes it.

## Is the forty-five day protection a ceramic coating?

No. It is our own protection blend, applied at the end of the detail, and it is topped up on a later visit rather than cured onto the paint. If you want protection measured in years, look at [ceramic coating](/services/ceramic-coating) instead.

## How long does a Gold Package take?

Longer than the other two, and it varies a lot with the state of the interior. Rather than quote a number that may not hold for your vehicle, we confirm the time window with you when the appointment is booked.
