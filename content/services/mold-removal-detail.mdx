{config.displayName} clears mold and mildew out of vehicle interiors for owners in {config.primaryCity} and across the Treasure Coast. It is shop work: treating the surfaces and then getting the interior dry needs the vehicle somewhere controlled.

## What is included

The affected surfaces are treated, moisture is extracted from the carpets, padding and upholstery, and the interior is deodorized. An ozone treatment is part of the job, which is what deals with what is left in the air and the vents rather than only what is on the seats.

## Why it happens here

Moisture that never fully dried. A window left open through an afternoon storm, a seal that leaks a little, a spill that went into the carpet rather than onto it. Anywhere else that might sit harmlessly for a week; in Florida humidity it starts growing.

## Will it come back

Not if the water stops arriving. Mold grows back wherever moisture keeps getting in, so if the cause is a leaking seal, a blocked air conditioning drain or a sunroof channel, that has to be dealt with or the interior will go the same way again. We are not doing that repair, but we will tell you where it looked like the water was coming from.

## Mold or water damage

If the vehicle has been through standing water, start with the [water damage detail](/services/water-damage-detail). If it was damp rather than flooded and now smells, this is the one.
