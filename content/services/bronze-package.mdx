{config.displayName} brings the Bronze Package to your driveway in {config.primaryCity} and across the Treasure Coast. It is our entry level detail, starting at one hundred twenty-five, and it covers an exterior wash, tire shine, an interior vacuum and a wipe down.

## What is included in the Bronze Package?

An exterior wash, tire shine, a vacuum through the cabin, and a wipe down of the interior surfaces. It is built to keep a vehicle that is already in reasonable shape looking sharp. If the inside needs real work, or the paint needs more than a wash, the Silver and Gold packages go further.

## Do you come to me?

Yes. All three detailing packages are mobile. We arrive with the equipment and detail the vehicle where it sits, so you are not dropping anything off or waiting anywhere. Tell us where the vehicle will be parked when you book and we will confirm anything we need on site.

## How often should I book one?

As general maintenance, a wash and vacuum roughly once a month keeps dirt, salt and sand from building into the paint and the carpets. How often you actually need it comes down to where the vehicle is parked, how close you are to the water, and how far you drive.

## Should I book Bronze or Silver?

Bronze maintains a car that is already relatively clean. Choose Silver when you want the paint itself to look its best: it adds an exterior wax and a light interior detail on top of everything here. Either way the starting prices are on the [booking page](/book).
