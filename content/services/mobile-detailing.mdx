{config.displayName} brings all three detailing packages to your driveway in {config.primaryCity} and across the Treasure Coast. We arrive with the equipment, water and power we need and work on the vehicle where it is parked, so nobody drops anything off and nobody waits anywhere.

The three packages are the same job at three depths. Bronze is the maintenance level: washed outside, wheels and tires cleaned, vacuumed through and wiped down inside, glass done both sides. Silver adds protection and the interior surfaces: wax or a sealant over all the paint, tire dressing, door jambs, and the plastics, vinyl and leather cleaned and protected. Gold goes after what the first two leave: the paint is decontaminated with an iron treatment and clay where it needs it, and the interior is taken down to the vents, seams, cup holders, pedals, headliner and under the seats.

## What is an add-on rather than part of a package

A seat shampoo, pet hair removal and an ozone odor treatment are add-ons at a price each, not something any package quietly includes. That is worth being plain about, because a vehicle with a dog in it every day needs the add-on and not a bigger package, and it costs less that way round.
