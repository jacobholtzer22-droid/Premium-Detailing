## What does paint protection film actually do?

It takes the damage instead of your paint. Rock chips, scratches and road debris hit the film, and the top layer is self healing, so fine scratches and swirls fade out with heat or sunlight rather than staying in the surface. The factory finish underneath is preserved, which is what protects the value of the vehicle.

## Should I get film or a ceramic coating?

They solve different problems. Film stops physical damage. A [ceramic coating](/services/ceramic-coating) adds gloss, chemical resistance and much easier cleaning, but it is far too thin to stop a stone. Many owners do both: film across the front and the other impact areas, coating over everything.

## Can you see the film once it is on?

It is applied to be nearly invisible, and it is available in gloss or matte depending on the look you are after. Done properly it reads as paint, not as a layer on top of it.
