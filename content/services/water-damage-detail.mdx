{config.displayName} takes flood and leak damage out of vehicle interiors for owners across {config.primaryCity} and the Treasure Coast. The work is done at the shop, because getting an interior genuinely dry takes somewhere controlled and a day or two of sitting there.

## What is included

Moisture is extracted from the carpets, the padding underneath them and the upholstery. Mold and mildew are treated, the interior is deodorized, and an ozone treatment is part of the job. Then it is dried out properly rather than left to air.

## What is not included

Electrical, mechanical and structural repairs are outside this service. If water reached a module or a harness, that is a job for a shop that does electrical work, and we will tell you what we saw and where it looked like the water was getting in.

## Why the padding matters more than the carpet

The carpet is the part you can see and the part that dries first. The padding underneath holds water for weeks, which is where the smell comes from and where mold starts. Pulling the carpet back to get at it is the difference between an interior that is dry and one that only looks dry.

## How soon should it be done

As soon as you can manage it. Florida humidity means standing water in a vehicle does not sit still, it grows, and every day it sits is more of the interior that has to come apart to deal with it. If it has already started to smell, the [mold removal detail](/services/mold-removal-detail) is the one you want.
