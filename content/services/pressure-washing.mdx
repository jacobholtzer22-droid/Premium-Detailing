{config.displayName} washes driveways, pavers and house exteriors at properties across {config.primaryCity} and the Treasure Coast. This one happens at your place, the same way the detailing packages do, so there is nothing to arrange beyond letting us at a water supply.

## What gets washed

A driveway, a paver patio or walkway, or the exterior of the house itself. Pressure is matched to the surface: concrete takes a great deal more than painted siding, a screen enclosure or a pool deck, which is why the driveway and the house are priced and handled as different jobs.

## What paver sealing does

Pavers get washed first, then the sealer goes on over the cleaned surface. It slows down how quickly they stain and how quickly the algae comes back, which in this climate is the difference between washing them every year and washing them every few. It is priced by the square foot, because the only thing that moves that number is how much ground there is.

## Why it needs doing more often down here

Shade, humidity and summer rain are what algae wants, and a north-facing driveway under a tree gets there faster than anything else on the property. It is the same reason a vehicle parked under a tree needs detailing more often than one that is not.

## Can it be done with a detail

Yes, and it is worth asking. If we are already at the house for a [detailing package](/services/mobile-detailing), doing the driveway on the same visit saves a second trip for everybody.
