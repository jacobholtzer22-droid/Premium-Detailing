## What does window tint actually do?

It cuts the heat and glare coming through the glass, which is the reason most people around here ask for it. It also blocks the ultraviolet light that fades a dashboard, cracks a trim panel and ages leather, so it protects the interior as much as it improves the drive. Privacy and the look of the vehicle come with it.

## Is window tint legal in Florida?

Florida sets limits on how much light each window has to let through, and those limits are different for the windshield, the front sides and the rear. We tint to the legal limit, so the vehicle stays road legal.

## How soon can I roll the windows down?

Freshly applied film needs a few days to cure before the windows are moved, and moving them early is the most common way a new tint job gets ruined. We will tell you at the appointment exactly how long to leave them alone for your vehicle.

## Does it help with how hot a car gets here?

Yes, and that is usually the point. A vehicle parked in full Florida sun gains most of its heat through the glass, so cutting what comes through the windows is the single most effective thing you can do about it short of parking in shade.
