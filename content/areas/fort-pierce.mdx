## Detailing in {area.name}

{area.name} sits on the Indian River Lagoon with a working inlet out to the Atlantic, and that proximity to salt water is the main thing that shapes how vehicles age here. Salt carries inland on the breeze, settles on paint and sits there until it is washed off, which is why a vehicle parked near the water needs attention more often than the same vehicle a few miles west.

## What we do in {area.name}

{config.displayName} brings the Bronze, Silver and Gold packages to driveways throughout {area.name} and the rest of {area.county}. For boat trailers and work trucks that live close to the inlet, the paint protection side of what we do matters more than the wash: a [ceramic coating](/services/ceramic-coating) gives salt and contamination far less to bond to, and makes the regular rinse-off considerably easier.
